# Evidence for each technique

The research behind each technique in the `explain` skill.

| Technique | Source | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Learner-paced segments | Rey et al. (2019), meta-analysis of 56 studies | Small to medium gains in retention and transfer |
| Key terms first | Mayer (2009), pre-training principle | Better transfer in 5 of 5 tests, median d = 0.85 |
| Remove extra information | Mayer, coherence principle | Better learning in 23 of 23 tests, median d = 0.86 |
| Map and bold key sentence | Alpizar et al. (2020); Richter et al. (2016) | d = 0.38; larger benefit for readers with low prior knowledge |
| Explicit connections | McNamara et al. (1996); McNamara & Kintsch (1996) | Low-knowledge readers understand high-cohesion texts better; the effect reverses for high-knowledge readers |
| Worked examples, correct only, no added self-explanation | Barbieri et al. (2023), meta-analysis of 55 studies | Moderate effect; correct examples were better than incorrect examples; added self-explanation prompts gave lower performance |
| Subgoal labels | Margulieux & Catrambone (2016); Margulieux et al. (2020) | Better problem solving in programming |
| Refutation of misconceptions | Schroeder & Kucera (2022), 44 comparisons, n = 3,869 | g = 0.41 |
| Analogies and case comparison | Alfieri et al. (2013), 57 experiments | Medium effect; focus on similarities was better |
| "Why" and "explain" questions | Bisra et al. (2018), 64 studies, about 6,000 participants | Better learning than no prompts or instructor explanation only |
| Questions after each segment and review after 1-6 days | Adesope et al. (2017), 272 effect sizes | Practice tests are better than restudy; the largest effects were with 1-6 days between practice and final test |
| No "Do you understand?" | Dunlosky & Lipko (2007) | Correlation between judged and real comprehension is about 0.27 |
| Less help for advanced users | Kalyuga et al. (2003), expertise reversal effect | Help that is necessary for novices can decrease learning for experts |
| ASD-STE100 | Chervak, Drury & Ouellette (1996); Shubert et al. (1995); Eckert (1997) | Significantly better comprehension of aircraft workcards, mostly for difficult texts and non-native readers; other studies show a positive trend that is not significant |
